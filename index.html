Know what the Sun is up to

Heliotide is a weather app for space. It tells you when a magnetic storm comes, when to look up for the aurora, and what just shook the ground. Free. No ads, no account.

Data: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, NASA, USGS, GDACS, Environment and Climate Change Canada and Apple Weather. Pictures of the Sun: courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams. Heliotide is an independent app. These organisations do not endorse it.

Heliotide shows what is measured and forecast. It is not medical advice and it is not an official warning service. In an emergency, follow your local authorities.
