Heliotide Privacy Policy

Where does the data come from?

From official public sources such as NOAA (space weather, aurora, tsunami bulletins), USGS (earthquakes), GDACS (cyclones, floods, volcanoes, wildfires), NASA and ESA (pictures of the Sun), NASA Earth Observatory (pictures of natural events). The weather comes from Apple Weather. You can see a list in the app under Settings, Data sources.

Why is a value missing or old?

Heliotide loads its data from these sources over the internet. Sometimes a source is late or doesn't answer, and sometimes the phone has no connection. The bottom of Today shows when the data was updated and which sources did not answer. Pull down on Today to try again. When you are offline, the app says "Reconnecting…", and what you see may be old.

Values "near you" follow your location. If the place is wrong, tap the gear on Today, then Location, and use your current location or choose a city.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

On Today, tap the gear, then Notifications, then Notify me. There you also choose which kinds of events you want to hear about. For many of them you choose how strong they must be, and how near to you. If you said no to notifications before, the same screen takes you to your iPhone or iPad settings.

Notifications need a device signed in to iCloud.

Alerts can be late and can be missed, so please don't rely on Heliotide for your safety. In danger, follow your local authorities.

How do I report or hide someone in the chat?

Touch and hold their message, then tap Hide this person. You stop seeing their messages, and a report goes to the moderator. They are not told. To see them again, tap the gear on Today, then Chat, and look under Hidden people.

You can also report a message by email. Tell me the room, the time and the name or code next to the message.

How do I delete my chat data?

On Today, tap the gear, then Chat, then Delete my chat data. This deletes your messages and reactions for everyone, in all rooms, along with your chat name and your list of hidden people. The app also tries to delete the reports you sent. If iCloud does not allow it, they stay, and only the moderator can read them. Words that others quoted from you stay in their messages. It can't be undone.

To delete one message, touch and hold it and tap Delete.

Deleting the app does not delete your chat messages, so do this first. If it did not finish, or the app is already gone, write to me.
