Data Sources

Heliotide shows data from official public sources. It changes nothing in them and makes no own forecasts.

Space weather

  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center: K-index and its forecast, solar flares, solar wind, alerts, the NOAA scales, the aurora forecast (OVATION) and the WSA-Enlil solar wind model.
  • Pictures of the Sun: courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams.

Earth

  • USGS: earthquakes.
  • GDACS: cyclones, floods, volcanoes and wildfires.
  • NOAA Tsunami Warning Centers: tsunami bulletins.
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada: rain radar for Canada.
  • Relief on this website: NASA/METI ASTER GDEM.

Weather

The weather comes from Apple Weather. Data sources of Apple Weather.

Please note

Heliotide is an independent app. These organisations do not endorse it.

Heliotide shows what is measured and forecast. It is not medical advice and it is not an official warning service. In an emergency, follow your local authorities.
